# Leagues is the best thing about OSRS

Leagues is a temporary game mode in [Old School Runescape](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Leagues). The premise is a short-term, restricted game mode where you progressively unlock powerful boosts that take you from a fresh account to end game content very rapidly. The game mode typically features boosted experience rates, boosted drop chances for rare items, and some form of combat tweaks that change the way you play the game, often in broken ways.

In order to unlock buffs, game regions, and further increased experience rates, you complete tasks in the game. Your route for tasks, your region selections, and your selection of buffs make each player's play through a unique experience. The process of planning and optimization is the main draw for me, and I try to plan out a route for myself that is achievable in the amount of time I can dedicate to the game during the league. 

I've played every leagues in OSRS, and the first League was the reason I made an [ironman](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Ironman_Mode) in the game. The previous league, [Raging Echoes](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Raging_Echoes_League), was the first time I went for and achieved Dragon rank. Dragon rank is the highest tier of ranking in leagues, but it's not as competitive as it used to be. In earlier leagues, Dragon rank was only allotted to the top x% of players, meaning it was a moving target. As players progressed, and competed for dragon rank, the number of task points required to get to dragon rank increased, making it more difficult to achieve over time. A benefit (or maybe drawback) of this system was that you had to keep grinding tasks over time in order to actually get dragon rank - you didn't just get your point threshold and then call it quits.

With the last couple of leagues, Dragon rank has been achievable at a static point threshold. This means once you've reached it, you have the rank locked in and don't necessarily need to keep grinding tasks. This also has the huge benefit of allowing players to route out tasks in advance, knowing which tasks can be skipped and which tasks to complete in order to save time to get to the rank as fast as possible.

## Region Selections

This league, [echo items](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Demonic_Pacts_League/Echo_bosses_and_echo_equipment) synergized incredibly well with [demonic pacts](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Demonic_Pacts_League/Demonic_Pacts), which meant that your region selection was important to optimize your build.

I went with the following regions, in order:
- [Kandarin](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Demonic_Pacts_League/Areas#Kandarin)
- [Tirannwn](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Demonic_Pacts_League/Areas#Tirannwn)
- [Desert](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Demonic_Pacts_League/Areas#Desert)

Kandarin allowed me to make fast progression early on, as the region is mostly full of mid-level content. Tirannwn gave me access to the Corrupted Gauntlet early. My early build allowed me to clear CG consistently and easily, and even complete my first echo boss. This allowed me to snowball more demonic pacts quickly and flesh out more of my build quickly. I also got access to full crystal armor, the crystal blessing which let the armor boost melee gear as well as magic, and even a crystal halberd. I spec'ed into the halberd build, making the crystal halberd a 5-tick weapon with crystal armor buffs with very few pact points. An extremely efficient early ish build.

Lastly, I went Desert. I did not pick this region in the Raging Echoes league, and so hadn't fought echo KQ, so this gave me an opportunity to try that out. I also wanted **some** raid region, and prefer ToA to the other raids, so this was a decent choice for me. Looking back, though, I think Desert wasn't the best region choice. The content and gear you get from the region isn't really great with my final build. Morytania would have given me significantly better tank gear, or even Zeah would have further improved skilling methods instead.

## Pacts Builds

- [Initial pacts for CG rush](https://tools.runescape.wiki/demonic-pacts/?n=1-133-44-46-53-56-59-68)
  - Powered staffs + water healing
  - Great inside CG, outside of CG you need to get the warped scepter for this to be any good
- [Pacts for grinding CG](https://tools.runescape.wiki/demonic-pacts/?n=1-133-153-154-155-156-43-44-46-53-56-59-68-74-83-88)
  - Halberd + powered staff + water healing
  - Trivializes CG without needing prayer penetration
  - Allows for using crystal halberd outside of CG for melee content
- [Re-spec for Prayer penetration](https://tools.runescape.wiki/demonic-pacts/?n=1-101-112-113-114-122-133-153-154-155-156-164-43-44-46-56-59-60-68-74-83-87-88-91)
  - Further trivialized echo CG
  - Good for demonic gorillas, progressing to further pacts
- [Final Build: Water + Air Mage + Thorns](https://tools.runescape.wiki/demonic-pacts/?n=1-10-100-107-108-109-11-112-113-114-122-133-135-136-139-140-141-145-44-45-46-55-56-58-59-6-60-67-68-7-71-74-8-81-84-9-93-96-97-99)
  - Air mage is huge DPS, with guaranteed chance to max hit
  - Water + Thorns can fully AFK the inferno up to Zuk
  - Overall incredibly overpowered build
