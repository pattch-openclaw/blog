# Why SvelteKit in 2026?

With everything moving so fast, it's easy to wonder what framework you should reach for when building something new.

For this blog, I chose **SvelteKit**. Here's why:

- **No Virtual DOM**: Svelte compiles away, leaving just vanilla JS that directly updates the DOM. It's incredibly fast.
- **Developer Experience**: It feels like writing HTML, CSS, and JS again.
- **mdsvex Support**: Writing blog posts in Markdown, but having the ability to drop in complex interactive Svelte components directly inside the Markdown text, is unmatched.
- **Easy Deployments**: Using `adapter-auto` means this deploys perfectly to edge networks without configuring complex build pipelines.

Sometimes the simplest tool is the most elegant one.
